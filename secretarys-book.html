Questions

Frequently asked

What do I need to use it?

Microsoft Excel on Windows or Mac. It's designed for Excel, so we recommend Excel for the best experience, including the colour-coded flags and dropdowns.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. You type in the cream cells and everything else works itself out. The formulas are protected so nothing breaks by accident, and the Start Here tab walks you through set-up.

Does it replace Play Football or our registration platform?

No, it works alongside it. Official player registrations stay in your registration platform. The Secretary's Book is the secretary's own admin record: meetings, decisions, actions, compliance, volunteers, sponsors and handover.

Does it tell me the rules for WWCC, AGMs and annual returns?

It gives you fields, typical checklists and reminders so nothing slips through. Rules differ by state and by your club's constitution, so always confirm the current requirements with your state agency, member federation and state regulator. It's an organising tool, not legal advice.

Can our whole committee use it?

Yes. One purchase covers one club, so you can share the file with your committee. Please don't share it with other clubs. For multiple clubs, ask us about an association licence.

Is there a version for other sports?

The Football Edition comes first, and editions for other sports are on the way. Get notified when yours is ready.
