Refunds, licence & privacy

For Paperbark (delete this box before going live): this is plain-English starter wording. Read it carefully and change it to match how you run your business. If you're unsure, get it checked.

Refunds

Our products are digital downloads, so you receive them straight away after purchase.

If a file is faulty, won't open, or isn't as described, email us and we'll fix it, replace it or refund you. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law aren't affected by anything on this page.

If you've simply changed your mind after downloading, we're generally unable to offer a refund, but please get in touch. We're always happy to help you get the book working for you.

Licence: what you can do with your download

  • Club and business books: one purchase covers one club, business or household. You can share the file with your own committee, staff or family.
  • Please don't share, resell or upload the files for other clubs, businesses or the public.
  • Associations, councils and networks that want to share a book with several clubs or members can ask us about a licence.

Not professional advice

Our books help you organise information. They aren't legal, financial, tax or compliance advice. Requirements such as child-related checks, AGM rules, insurance and annual returns vary by state and change over time, so always confirm them with the relevant authority.

Privacy

When you buy, our checkout provider (Payhip) collects the details needed to process your order and deliver your download, such as your name, email and payment details. We use your email to send your purchase and any product updates. If you join our "notify me" list, we use your email only to tell you about new or updated books, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

We don't sell your information. The information you type into our books stays in your own file on your own device. We can't see it.

Contact

Questions about an order? Email us and we'll get back to you.
